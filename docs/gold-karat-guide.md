# Gold Karat Guide

Gold karat describes the proportion of pure gold contained in a gold alloy.

Pure gold is generally represented as 24K. Lower-karat gold contains a mixture of gold and other metals that can increase durability and change the appearance of the material.

## Common Gold Karats

### 10K Gold

10K gold contains approximately 41.7% gold. It is commonly used for jewelry because the additional alloy metals can make it relatively durable.

### 14K Gold

14K gold contains approximately 58.3% gold. It is a common jewelry purity and offers a balance between gold content and durability.

### 18K Gold

18K gold contains approximately 75% gold. It has a higher gold content than 14K and is frequently used for fine jewelry.

### 22K Gold

22K gold contains approximately 91.7% gold. Because of its high gold content, it is softer than lower-karat alloys.

### 24K Gold

24K represents nearly pure gold, although commercial 24K gold may be around 99.9% pure. It is generally softer than gold alloys with lower karat values.

## Estimating Gold Value

A simplified calculation is:

```text
Weight × (Karat ÷ 24) × 24K Price Per Gram
```

For example, if an item weighs 5 grams, is 18K, and the 24K gold price is $100 per gram:

```text
5 × (18 ÷ 24) × 100 = $375
```

The result is an estimate of the underlying gold content.

## Market Prices

The actual amount paid for gold can differ from a calculated metal value. Jewelry may include manufacturing costs, retailer margins, taxes, design charges, and other fees.
